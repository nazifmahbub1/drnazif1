Online Health Consultation

As a practicing General Physician (MBBS) and Public Health Consultant (MPH), Dr. Nazif provides online health consultations for patients in Bangladesh. From the comfort of your home, you can talk through your symptoms, ongoing conditions, test results, and everyday health concerns with a qualified doctor over a secure video call, and walk away with clear, practical guidance on what to do next.

WhatsApp video consultationFor patients in BangladeshBangla or English
What you can consult about

Everyday care, from wherever you are

  • General health concerns and common illnesses such as fever, cough, cold, infections, stomach and digestive issues, and skin problems
  • Ongoing conditions such as diabetes, high blood pressure, and thyroid problems, including regular follow-up
  • Review and plain-language explanation of your test and lab results
  • Advice on medicines you are already taking, and prescriptions where appropriate
  • Guidance on diet, lifestyle, and preventive health
  • A clear next-step plan, including when and where to seek in-person care or a specialist referral

Good to know

Online consultation is best suited to non-emergency concerns. If you have a medical emergency such as severe chest pain, difficulty breathing, heavy bleeding, or signs of a stroke, please go to your nearest hospital right away. Some conditions need a physical examination or tests, and in those cases Dr. Nazif will guide you to the right in-person care.
