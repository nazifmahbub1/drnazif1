Speaking topics

The five families of soft skills

Every talk draws on this map - pick one family for a focused session, or the full arc for a keynote.

Communication

  • Why clarity beats vocabulary and accent, every time
  • The Headline Method: answer first, support briefly, then stop talking
  • Listening as a trainable skill, not a personality trait
  • Writing and presenting ideas so people actually retain them

Emotional Intelligence

  • Noticing your own emotions in real time, before they drive your actions
  • Reading a room and responding wisely under pressure
  • The 80/20 Reflect: how genuine listening de-escalates tension
  • Why EQ predicts life outcomes as strongly as raw intelligence

Leadership & Influence

  • Leadership as behaviour, not a job title
  • Making decisions without endless second-guessing
  • Giving honest feedback kindly, and staying composed under pressure
  • Moving people toward an outcome without force

Collaboration & Relationships

  • Working smoothly with people whose styles clash with yours
  • Addressing disagreement directly instead of letting it rot in silence
  • Setting boundaries - and hearing 'no' without taking offence
  • The CLEAR path for difficult conversations people keep postponing

Professional Effectiveness

  • Speaking up in meetings and interviews with a trained, steady voice
  • Negotiating a deadline, a salary, or a scope with confidence
  • Presenting yourself professionally, in person and online
  • Turning soft skills into visible career results
